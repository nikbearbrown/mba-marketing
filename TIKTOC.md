# Principles of Marketing: with LLMs
## Compact Tik TOC Architecture

**Book type:** MBA / marketing textbook  
**Status:** Retrospective architecture for developed content  

## Core Thesis

This book teaches marketing as customer understanding translated into value creation, positioning, communication, channels, measurement, and ethical growth, with LLMs used for research and creative iteration under strategic control.

## Primary Reader

MBA students, marketing students, founders, product managers, and communications professionals.

## Learning Arc

1. **Market understanding:** customers, segmentation, targeting, positioning, and value.
2. **Marketing execution:** product, price, place, promotion, brand, content, and channels.
3. **Measurement and strategy:** analytics, experiments, growth, ethics, AI, and integrated plans.

## Chapter Architecture

Each chapter should begin with a market decision, use evidence to frame the customer, then produce a strategic artifact.

## Terminal Capability

The reader can build a coherent marketing plan, critique AI-generated marketing outputs, and connect tactics to customer value.

## Adoption Risks

- AI copy can substitute for strategy.
- Platform examples age quickly.
- Ethical use of customer data must be visible throughout.
